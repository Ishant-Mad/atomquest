# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Managers, employees, and HRs of various organizations. As a hackathon submission, the primary audience includes judges who expect a highly intuitive, seamless, and immediate demo experience.

## Product Purpose
A digitized Goal Setting & Tracking Portal designed to streamline performance management. Its primary goal as a hackathon project is to showcase an extraordinary, intuitive, and demo-ready interface that highlights the value proposition rapidly.

## Brand Personality
Professional, transparent, structured, and modern with an emphasis on a highly polished, intuitive demo experience (similar to premium B2B SaaS).

## Anti-references
Absolutely no "AI slop dashboards" with endless nested cards. Avoid generic, cluttered legacy HR software looks.

## Design Principles
1. **Demo-Optimized**: Workflows must be instantly comprehensible and highly visual for a fast-paced hackathon pitch.
2. **Clarity over Density**: Information should be readable at a glance without clutter.
3. **Show, Don't Tell**: Let the interface and interactions explain the product's value quickly and clearly.
4. **Action-Oriented**: Goal status and next steps should be immediately obvious with high impact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG compliance. Clear contrast for text, and clear error states.
